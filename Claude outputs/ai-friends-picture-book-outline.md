# Picture Book Outline: Friends Who Meet to Learn AI

Prompts for your own writing. Not the story itself.

---

## Page 1: The Monthly Get-Together

**Beat:** A bunch of friends come together, as they do once a month, to learn AI.

**Nudges:**
- How do you want to open? With the day on the calendar, the friends arriving, or the moment they sit down together?
- What does "once a month" feel like to them? Something they wait for, or something that just happens?
- How many friends do you want to show? Will you name them, or keep them as "some of us"?

**Illustration idea:** The friends gathering in one space, each one arriving with their own thing.

---

## Page 2: Everyone Makes Something Different

**Beat:** Each friend works on their own thing: software, static websites, a portfolio, visuals from music.

**Nudges:**
- Do you want one friend per project, or show the group moving between them?
- Which project do you want the reader to linger on? Maybe the music visuals, since it's the most visual?
- Could you use a simple repeated line for each one? For example: "Some of us make..."

**Illustration idea:** A spread split into little scenes: code, a website, a portfolio, music turning into pictures.

---

## Page 3: A Space to Play and Fail

**Beat:** There are no rules here. It's a space to play around and to fail.

**Nudges:**
- How will you show failing? Something that breaks, a funny mistake, or a shrug and a restart?
- Is failing a big moment or a small, light one? What tone feels true to your group?
- Do you want to end on the idea of "no constraints," or on the friends coming back next month?

**Illustration idea:** The friends laughing over something that didn't work, with the room loose and messy.
